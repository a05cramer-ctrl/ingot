03the route

Three steps, and none of them
are held by us.

01
trade on pons
buys and sells of $ingot on the Pons bonding curve and market.
02
creator fee
Pons collects a fee on volume and credits it to the creator wallet.
03
routed to holders in $gld
the fee is swapped to $GLD and distributed. Each distribution is a transaction.

The creator-fee mechanism is a platform feature of Pons, not something ingot invented, controls, or can guarantee. Pons pays the fee to the creator wallet; ingot's chosen use of that fee is to convert it to $GLD and send it back out to holders. If volume stops, the fee is zero and nothing is routed. Read it in the Pons documentation.

06the pair

$GLD is not a
picture of gold.

SPDR Gold Shares is the largest physically backed gold exchange-traded fund in the world. It holds allocated bullion — numbered bars, assigned to the trust, not a claim on a pool — held in the London vaults of its custodian. The share price tracks the metal, less expenses.

ingot routes its fees into that, on chain. Read the fund's own material at spdrgoldshares.com.

07risk

the assetMemecoins routinely go to zero. $ingot is a memecoin. It has no revenue, no floor, no redemption, and no obligation to anybody. The gold pairing does not make it a gold product and does not protect the token's price. Assume the entire position can be lost.

the feesFee routing depends entirely on trading volume. Volume is not promised and is frequently zero. When there is no volume there is no fee, and when there is no fee nothing is routed. Past routings shown above say nothing about future ones. The mechanism is a Pons platform feature and may change or stop outside our control.

the restNothing here is investment advice, a solicitation, or an offer. No deposits are taken. No funds are held or managed on anyone's behalf. Do your own research, read the contract, and only ever risk what you can lose entirely.
